### What the data say about a page this size

- Company pages get lower reach than people. Metricool 2026 (673K posts): similar impressions per post but engagement 1.6% vs 2.6% for personal. Refine Labs and Ordinal report employee profiles out-reach brand pages by multiples (small or vendor samples).
- A page with under 100 followers sees ~150 impressions a post (Willow, 374 pages). Reaching ~250 per post takes roughly 1,000 followers.
- Consistent weekly posting beat bursts by 4.6x on follower growth (Willow, 488 B2B pages; vendor data, correlational).
- LinkedIn says complete pages get 30% more weekly views and weekly posting doubles engagement.
- Named-person posts gained +73% engagement in one small dataset (LigoSocial, 27 pages); generic "thought leadership" lost 24%.

**So the page's job at 0-500 followers is credibility and proof, not reach.** A buyer clicks from your post or DM, sees a live, specific page, and trusts you more. The profile stays the growth engine. Do not judge the page on reactions.
